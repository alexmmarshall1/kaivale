MazeTD Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

MazeTD is a tower defense game made by Kaivale Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. In short: we don't collect any of it.

No accounts

MazeTD has no accounts of its own. You don't sign up, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Game Center handles your identity

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game uses it for leaderboards and online matches. Game Center shows your Game Center nickname to the players you play with, and posts your scores to the MazeTD leaderboards. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't get your Apple ID, and we don't store anything Game Center gives the game.

When an online match starts, the game may ask for access to your Game Center friends list. It only uses the list on your device, to turn typed chat on by default when you're playing with friends. You can say no; typed chat then starts off, and you can turn it on for a match yourself.

Chat

  • Online matches have an optional chat. Messages go straight between the players' devices through Game Center's match connection. They never pass through, or get stored on, any server of ours.
  • Messages exist only in the memory of the devices in the match. They're deleted when the match ends, when you leave it, or when the app closes. Nothing is saved.
  • Bad words are masked automatically on your device, messages with slurs or harassment are removed, and offensive player names are shown as "Player" plus a short code.
  • You can mute, block or report any player. If you report someone, the game opens an email to us in your own mail app, filled in with that player's Game Center name and player ID, the time of the match, and their recent messages. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • You can switch chat to quick chat only, or turn it off, in Settings. If your device has Screen Time restrictions on communication or multiplayer, typed chat or all chat is turned off automatically.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, chat, game setup), your personal stats and your blocked players list are saved only on your device. Blocked players are stored by their Game Center player ID so they stay blocked in future matches. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

MazeTD has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We don't sell or share information, because we don't have any.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases go through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. The game only learns which items you own, from the App Store.

Children

MazeTD doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Chat follows the device's Screen Time settings, so parents can turn communication off.

Contact us

Questions, or a report you'd rather send yourself: email kaivale.support@gmail.com. You can also use Settings → Contact support in the game. We read every report and aim to respond within 24 hours.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
