Conquest privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Conquest is a game by Kaivale Studio. This policy covers the Conquest app for iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Conquest has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send any information about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings, your tutorial progress, which army skin you wear, and a copy of what you've bought, so purchases keep working offline. This stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Apple services the game uses

  • Game Center (optional) runs co-op matches and leaderboards. When you play co-op, your Game Center nickname, colour and army skin are shown to the people you're playing with, and your moves are sent to their devices so everyone's game stays the same. Leaderboard scores go to Game Center. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive it.
  • In-app purchases go through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details.
  • Sharing: if you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your result and opens the system share sheet. It's sent only where you choose to send it.

Children

Conquest collects no personal information from anyone, children included. Purchases follow your device's Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
